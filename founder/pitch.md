UNBLUFF is a placement-preparation website for engineering students.
Free: upload your resume and get an ATS score, have your resume claims questioned, and get a study plan built only from what you have proven. Your first result takes about ten minutes.
Pro: INR 99 a month, or INR 499 once for a six-month placement-season pass. It adds the AI mentor (hints, not answers), unlimited plan refreshes and the resume tools. Try Pro free for 7 days, no card needed, and get a full refund in the first 7 days if you do not want it.
Your resume and data stay yours: you can delete everything with one button, and nothing is sold or shared.
Nothing counts as progress unless it was checked. Running your code against hidden tests is coming later and is not available yet.
