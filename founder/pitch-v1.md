UNBLUFF is a placement-preparation website for engineering students.
It questions the claims on your resume, teaches DSA patterns and DBMS with graded quizzes, scores your resume for applicant-tracking systems, and builds a daily study plan only from what you have actually proven. An AI mentor gives hints instead of handing over answers.
Free to start. Pro is INR 299 a month: the AI mentor, the resume tools and unlimited plan refreshes.
Nothing counts as progress unless it was checked, so the plan can be short at first. Running your code against hidden tests is coming later and is not available yet.
