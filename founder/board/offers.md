# Offers lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- Dream outcome is concrete and high-stakes: a campus placement offer. The pain is real for pre-final and final-year Indian engineering students, and the placement calendar gives natural urgency that is real, not fake.
- The likelihood angle is the one genuine edge: the app only counts verified evidence and labels self-reported marks as such. In a market full of unproven promises, "no bluff" is a believable claim, and the name says it.
- The stack already covers many problems on the way to the outcome: resume claim audit, ATS-scored resume, DSA patterns (27), DBMS topics (12) with graded quizzes, mentor with deletable memory, job matching, and a plan built from verified records.
- Delivery cost is low for most features; only the Gemini calls (mentor, audit grading, plan assistant) cost money per use.
- Targets are easy to find in principle: college groups, YouTube/Instagram placement content, word of mouth.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood has no proof yet.** Nothing in the brief shows a single student improved their placement result. The offer promises an outcome but has no case, no number, no guarantee. Without proof, the verified-evidence pitch is a claim about itself.
2. **The core promise is partly undeliverable today.** Code judging has no execution service, the company-wise question list cannot be sold (no licence), and the resume PDF compiler is dev-only. The strongest "evidence" feature, hidden-test judging, does not run. Effort is also high: the student must do the audit, practice and retests.
3. **Price and buyer are unproven.** The Pro plan and INR 299 a month are assumptions. Whether students (or their parents) will pay, and the free-tier alternatives they already use, are unknown. The offer is a pile of features, not a named, guaranteed package. No payment system or legal entity exists, and there is no distribution yet.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which exact student, in which college tier, has this pain and will pay? What do they pay for placement prep now?
- What result can you show: any student whose audit score or interview outcome improved, and by how much?
- How fast is the first win in the first session, before any judge exists?
- What is the guarantee? Would you refund if the plan is followed and nothing improves?
- What is the one named package, and why would a student feel foolish saying no at INR 299 (or double)?
- How will the first 100 students arrive, and what does each cost?
- Is the market growing or flat? That figure is unknown to me and unsourced here.

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Ship code execution so verified judging works, and keep the rule that unverified results never count.
- Run a small cohort and report real before/after evidence (open question: what lift, measured how).
- Package one named offer with a guarantee, and test the price range from the panel with actual payments.
- Resolve licensing and legal entity, and get the first 100 users through campus channels, with the cost tracked.
