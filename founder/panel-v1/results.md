## What would flip a no

- A clear, readable privacy promise plus a free version that shows me real results on my own resume and plan before I ever have to pay.
- Proof that the resume tools and the coding tests get students shortlisted by companies that visit our campus, or a free trial of the Pro features.
- If my batch mates all sign up and say the AI mentor actually helped them get shortlisted, or if there were a one-time payment option for a semester.
- If I could use the daily study plan and a few mentor hints for free for a week and it clearly showed my weak topics and what to fix before placements.
- A free trial that shows real DBMS and DSA quizzes plus a resume check that clearly finds problems in my own resume, so I can see it is better than what I already use.
- If the free tier gave me a real plan and a resume ATS score that showed clear improvement after a week of use, and the mentor hints were clearly better than what I get from searching.
- If it showed clearly that it covers aptitude and the service-company test pattern better than the free sites I use, with a trial I could try before paying.
- Seeing a clear, checkable result that the resume ATS score or the graded quizzes got me shortlisted or caught mistakes that the free stuff missed, and being able to try Pro for free first.
- A free check that shows my real resume gaps and a plan built from what I have proven, with a clear note on what happens to my resume and data, before I pay anything.
- A clear, free trial that shows the resume ATS scoring and the mentor are clearly better than what I already use for free, with real results from people in my college.
- A free trial of the AI mentor and resume scoring that clearly beats what I already get for free, or a Pro price I can justify to my parents without a fight.
- A free trial of the Pro features long enough to see my resume score and a real study plan built from my own progress, and that it saves me hours compared with YouTube.
